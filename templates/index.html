How My Tiger Works

Keystroke Dynamics

Analyzes the timing patterns between your keystrokes to detect emotional states. This includes press-to-press intervals, dwell times, and flight times between keys.

AI Processing

Advanced machine learning algorithms process your typing patterns in real-time using both rule-based analysis and XGBoost models for accurate emotion detection.

Privacy First

Only timing data is analyzed - your actual text content never leaves your device. No personal information is stored or transmitted to external servers.

Real-time Results

Get instant feedback on your emotional state as you type naturally. The system continuously updates emotion predictions with sub-second latency.
